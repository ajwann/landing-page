Privacy

This site is a personal landing page. It has no accounts, forms or ads. It uses Google Analytics so Adam can see roughly how many people visit and what they find useful.

What's collected

Google Analytics records which pages are viewed, which sections are opened or closed, and which links are clicked. It also records general information about the device and browser, plus an approximate location (country or city). The site doesn't collect names or email addresses, and Google Analytics doesn't log or store IP addresses.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_5CHWXMVCVD, to tell one visit from another. They last up to two years. The site sets no other cookies.

Who sees it

Only Adam, in Google Analytics reports. The data is never sold or shared. Google processes it as described in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Opting out

Block cookies for this site, use a content blocker, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works the same either way.
