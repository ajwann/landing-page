Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Adam Wanninger ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you visit awanninger.com or use any application, site, or service that links to this policy (collectively, the "Services").

By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Services.

1. Projects Covered by This Policy

This policy is the single privacy policy for all projects operated by Adam Wanninger, including every application that uses awanninger.com as its Google OAuth domain. It currently covers:

  • awanninger.com, this personal landing page. It is a static site with no sign-in. The only data collected is the analytics described below.
  • cats-mcp, an MCP server providing live Charlotte Area Transit System bus and rail data. Its hosted HTTP transport uses Google only to sign you in, requesting the openid and email scopes and nothing else. Your email address is used to identify your session and to check it against an access list. The server issues its own tokens rather than passing Google's through, and the transit data it returns comes from public CATS feeds, not from your Google account.
  • Any other project operated by us that links to this policy or that names awanninger.com as its OAuth domain.

Where a project requests Google permissions beyond those listed above, the specific permissions are shown to you on Google's consent screen before you grant access, and the practices described in "Google User Data" below apply to all of them.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide directly. Information you submit when you contact us, sign in, or otherwise interact with the Services. For example, your name, email address, and the contents of any message you send us.
  • Account information from third-party sign-in. If you choose to sign in using a third-party identity provider such as Google, we receive the profile information that provider shares with us, subject to the permissions you approve. See "Google User Data" below.
  • Usage and device information. Information automatically collected when you use the Services, such as IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your visits.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Small data files stored on your device that help the Services function and help us understand how the Services are used.
